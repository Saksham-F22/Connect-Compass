ACCESSIBILITY SETTINGS

- High contrast: stronger text, borders and button contrast.
- Reduce motion: disables animation and transition effects.
- Clearer font: uses a system sans-serif font.
- Preferences are saved in this browser. Use Reset accessibility settings to restore defaults.
